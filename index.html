How to use this simulator

1. Adjust the six levers on the left to describe your team’s shipping style.  2. Click “Run Simulation” — the model runs 25 weeks of forward-Euler dynamics in your browser.  3. Your scenario appears as the bold dark line; the six colored lines are reference scenarios for comparison.  4. Read the recommendations at the bottom for specific next steps based on your settings.  Or load a preset to jump to a known scenario.
